Sleeping Beauties: update 2 for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
================================================================

This archive has every file that differs from your original project, so it works
whether or not you already applied the first update.

How to apply
------------
1. Unzip this archive on top of your SleepingBeauties folder (the one that already
   has node_modules). Say "yes" to replacing files.
2. If they are still there, delete these four old files. Nothing uses them any more:
     src/data/eagleStory.js
     src/hooks/useNarrator.js      (and the empty src/hooks folder)
     src/pages/eagle.css
     src/pages/landing.css
3. Run the site:
     npm run dev        then open http://localhost:5173
   No new npm packages were added, so there is nothing new to install.

What changed in this update
---------------------------
1. Real photos in the storybooks now always appear. The photo used to stay invisible
   when it had finished loading before the page was ready to notice.
2. Mission files open in a bright reading mode. The moon/sun button in the top bar
   switches between light and dark, and the choice is remembered.
3. Home page: Worlds now comes before Storybooks (in the page and in the menu).
4. Storybook wording: each book is now "A bedtime story from the Moon / Mars /
   deep space", with its reading time and page count shown as small labels. The
   narrator's cover line was re-recorded to match.
5. New storybook: Voyager 1, "The traveller who kept going"
     /voyager-1           13 pages, narrated (about 9 minutes), with its
                          own deep-space lullaby, real NASA photos and two NASA videos
     /voyager-1/mission   the mission file: timeline, the NASA 3D model with labelled
                          parts, a live map of where Voyager 1 is now (distance,
                          radio delay and kilometres flown while you read), what it
                          carries, photos, videos and sources
6. Home page ring: autoplay no longer stops after a while. It pauses only while you
   point at or tab to its buttons, or drag it, and then carries on by itself.
7. Worlds, Storybooks and Photos: headings now start right under the menu bar.
8. Every book has a way back to its world: Eagle to the Moon page, Viking 1 to the
   Mars page, Voyager 1 to the Space page (with Voyager 1 selected), plus a home
   button.
9. Space, Moon and Mars pages: not touched (their files are byte-for-byte unchanged).

See README.md for details, credits, and how to rebuild the narration.
